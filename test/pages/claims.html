A short history of deep learning milestones

Deep learning moved from a niche to the centre of AI in about a decade. This page collects the milestones people cite most often.

Vision

The ImageNet dataset contains more than 14 million hand-annotated images. In 2012, AlexNet won the ImageNet challenge with a top-5 error of about 15.3%, far ahead of the runner-up, and it was trained on two GPUs.

Games

In March 2016, DeepMind's AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol 4–1 in a five-game match in Seoul. A year earlier it had beaten the European champion Fan Hui.

Language

The Transformer was introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani and colleagues at Google. BERT followed in 2018 and was released by OpenAI as an open-source model.

GPT-3, released in 2020, has 1.5 billion parameters and was trained mostly on filtered web text. The Adam optimizer, used to train most of these models, was introduced by Kingma and Ba in 2014.

What it means

Whether these systems understand anything is still debated, and the answer depends on what we mean by understanding.
