Softmax and cross-entropy

A classifier outputs one score per class, called a logit. The softmax turns logits into probabilities:

\[ p_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}} \]

The loss

With the correct class \(y\), the cross-entropy loss is \(L = -\log p_y\), and its gradient with respect to the logits is \(\frac{\partial L}{\partial z_i} = p_i - \mathbb{1}[i = y]\).

This gradient is why softmax and cross-entropy are usually fused into one function.
