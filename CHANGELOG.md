# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (31 Oct 2026)

First public release.

- **Ask about a selection** in a thread with Markdown, tables and KaTeX maths; a pin in the margin brings it back.
- **Rewrite in place**, in the site's own style, with real maths; Again, Original and Undo beside every edit.
- **Ask about the whole page, or change it**: page-wide changes stream in three at a time, with Review and Undo all.
- **The browser's side panel**: the page shrinks beside it and it follows you from tab to tab (or keep it inside the page, in Settings).
- **Answers you can check**: page answers quote the page with numbered chips, each quote verified against the page; every answer shows the model, the exact cost and exactly what was sent.
- **Lecture companion**: ask about the moment in a video, from its captions and the frame on screen; timestamps jump the video.
- **Across your tabs**: compare the tabs you pick, every point linked to its quote in its own tab.
- **Catch me up**: a recap of what you've read so far, with nothing from further down.
- **Fact-check**: a page's key claims (or a selection's) checked with web search, tinted in place, sources in the panel.
- **Your own actions**: one-click prompts in the toolbar, made and changed by chatting or in Settings; JSON export and import.
- **Quiet memory and suggestions**: terms you've asked about before are recalled when you select them again; long pages you left part-way offer to take you back; "Stuck?" and "Recap" appear only when you keep coming back.
- **All notes**: every page you've asked about or changed, searchable, with Markdown export and JSON backup.
- **Fast routing with Jev**: questions start answering about a second sooner; web search and careful reasoning switch on when needed; pages with text aimed at AI assistants get a warning.
- **Security**: the composer and conversations run in extension frames or the side panel the page can't see into; page text is sent as marked, untrusted data; everything the model writes is sanitised; the key is readable only by the extension's worker. See SECURITY.md.
